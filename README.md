Our game is a PC-based flight simulator that balances realism with accessibility, drawing inspiration from the mission-based progression and short-session engagement of Dr. Driving. Instead of offering a fully open-ended professional simulator, the game focuses on distinct gameplay modes, each built around specific flight scenarios and challenges. These modes introduce difficulty not through complex controls or advanced simulation systems, but through contextual challenges, such as environmental conditions, time pressure, and situational constraints.
Players take on the role of a developing pilot completing structured flight missions. Each mission clearly communicates its objectives, rules, and evaluation criteria before takeoff. As players progress, they are introduced to new flight modes that require greater situational awareness, decision-making, and precision. This structure allows players to gradually develop skill while maintaining a strong sense of purpose and progression.


Use the following controls to fly your plane:
A / D - Roll
Q / E - Yaw
W / S - Pitch
Shift - Throttle Up
Ctrl - Throttle Down

Fly carefully and maintain control of your speed and altitude. Going too fast or making sharp turns can make it harder to pass through the rings or land smoothly. Once all rings have been collected, reduce your throttle and line up with the runway to perform a safe landing.

The goal is to complete the course as efficiently and accurately as possible. Practice will help improve your control and timing.
